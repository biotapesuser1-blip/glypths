# Core Concepts in SEBA Protocol

Seba Pool simplifies validator graduation and yield distribution.

## Validator Graduation
Solo validators graduate after six months, provided they meet key criteria. This unlocks perpetual yield as `sBOLD`, backed by the protocol's execution rewards.

## Boosts for Validators
Aligned participants receive reward boosts through:
- **Stakers Union Membership:** 2.5x rewards
- **Hero Socks POAP Holder:** 0.5x rewards

For maximum participation yields, participants should meet protocol-specific boosts during validator operations.
