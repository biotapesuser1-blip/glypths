# Vault System in SEBA Protocol

The Vault System enhances yield distribution and encourages graduated validators to optimize rewards.

## Working Principles
- Prior to graduation, all yield is automatically compounded.
- Post-graduation, validators choose between claiming rewards immediately or holding shares for potential long-term gains.

## Strategy for Graduates
- **Early Claim:** Secure initial rewards against market risks.
- **Long-term Holding:** Maximize yield as validator participation increases.
